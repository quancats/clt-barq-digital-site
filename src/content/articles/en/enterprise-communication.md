## Start with use cases

Identify where teams exchange information today and which channels they use for meetings, announcements, and daily conversations. Map duplication and fragmentation, then decide what to unify and what should remain separate.

## Review identity and permissions

Ask how accounts are created and removed when employees leave, how groups are managed, and how administrator roles are separated. Understand the organization's and vendor's responsibilities for configuration and monitoring.

## Understand data location and protection

Identify where content, metadata and backups are processed. Go beyond the word “encryption”: ask about its scope, key management and administrator access.

## Test before scaling

Select a small group representing different departments and use cases. Define usability, reliability, support and integration criteria, then review the results before expanding.
