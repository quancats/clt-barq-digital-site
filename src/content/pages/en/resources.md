      title: 'Enterprise communication platform evaluation checklist'
      body: |-
          Identify users, departments, locations and primary use cases.
          Inventory messaging, meetings, identity tools and systems requiring integration.
          Define information types, location, access and retention policies.
          Review protection features, scope, available evidence and operational requirements.
          Define a limited pilot, acceptance criteria and success indicators.
          Agree pricing, support, responsibilities, and migration or exit plans.
    - key: 'transformation'
      title: 'Digital transformation project planning checklist'
      body: |-
          Define the business objective, current process and problems.
          Identify process owner, stakeholders and users.
          Inventory systems, data, interfaces and dependencies.
          Prioritize requirements, constraints, security and privacy.
          Agree deliverables, timeline, budget and acceptance criteria.
          Plan training, operations, maintenance and impact measurement.
