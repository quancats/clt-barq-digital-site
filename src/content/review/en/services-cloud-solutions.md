# Cloud Solutions

Choosing a cloud environment requires balancing flexibility, cost, security and data requirements.

## When is this service relevant?

When your organization is seeking to improve its approach to cloud solutions, or is planning system changes and needs clear requirements before implementation. We start by reviewing the current environment, objectives, and dependencies.

## Potential scope

- Review current hosting
- Identify storage and compute needs
- Assess cloud and backup options
- Plan migration and access management

## Deliverables

Hosting recommendations, resource requirements, migration plan and operational risks.

## Our approach

We identify needs and constraints, present a clear scope for review, and agree implementation, handover, and responsibilities. Deliverables depend on project size and actual requirements; activities outside the agreed proposal are not included.
