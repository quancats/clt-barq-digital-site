# Barq Digital | Technology shaped by organizational needs

We are a Saudi establishment working in cybersecurity, information technology, and digital transformation, with a focus on enterprise solutions and products that connect protection, innovation, and operational value.

## Who we are

Successful technology is more than a set of tools. It is an environment that supports the way organizations work and helps them meet their objectives. We view protection, infrastructure, applications, and integration as interconnected disciplines.

## Our vision

To contribute to a more secure and trustworthy digital future, and to become a technology partner for organizations seeking to advance with confidence.

## Our mission

To deliver technology solutions and products that address organizational needs while balancing security, efficiency, and innovation through structured work and clear accountability for deliverables.

## Our values

- **Trust:** Clarity and transparency in communication and commitments.
- **Security:** Consideration of risks and privacy in technology decisions.
- **Quality:** Focus on usability and verification of deliverables.
- **Innovation:** Applying technology to real-world challenges.
- **Responsibility:** Respect for scope, data rights, and ownership.

## From services to products

BIM reflects Barq's direction in enterprise technology products. Rather than addressing isolated needs alone, we aim to build connected experiences that help organizations manage work and communication more effectively.

## How we work

1. Understand organizational objectives and the current environment.
2. Identify needs, priorities, and risks.
3. Design the solution and define deliverables and responsibilities.
4. Implement and integrate within agreed scope.
5. Verify, hand over, and define support requirements.
