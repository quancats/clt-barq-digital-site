# Cloud or on-premises? How to compare deployment options

Deployment is an operational, security and commercial decision shaped by organizational requirements.

## Define constraints and requirements

Review data location, connectivity, availability, infrastructure management capacity and required integrations. Determine what must remain under organizational control and what may be delegated.

## Understand total cost

Cost extends beyond hosting to operations, monitoring, backups, updates, incident response, scaling and future migration.

## Compare responsibilities

In each model, identify who manages systems, networks, keys, updates and support. Do not assume on-premises deployment is automatically safer or that cloud removes customer responsibilities.

## Decide based on evaluation

Review the proposed model with specialists and test use cases, recovery and integration. Document requirements and commitments in the proposal and contract.
