# Digital Transformation & Automation

Digital transformation starts with understanding how work happens, not adding tools without a clear purpose.

## When is this service relevant?

When your organization is seeking to improve its approach to digital transformation & automation, or is planning system changes and needs clear requirements before implementation. We start by reviewing the current environment, objectives, and dependencies.

## Potential scope

- Analyze current processes
- Identify duplication and manual work
- Design digital workflows
- Assess systems and integrations
- Define implementation stages

## Deliverables

Transformation priorities, workflow design, implementation scope and agreed evaluation measures.

## Our approach

We identify needs and constraints, present a clear scope for review, and agree implementation, handover, and responsibilities. Deliverables depend on project size and actual requirements; activities outside the agreed proposal are not included.
