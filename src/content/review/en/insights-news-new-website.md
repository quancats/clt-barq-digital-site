# Barq Digital launches its new corporate website

Barq Digital introduces a new digital experience showcasing the organization, its solutions, services, and BIM product.

## A clearer way to discover Barq

The website is designed to make it easier to explore technology capabilities and products and contact the team, with Arabic and English content and dedicated consultation and demo request paths.

## BIM in Barq's portfolio

The site includes an introduction to BIM for enterprise communication and collaboration, links to its independent website, and a demo request path.
