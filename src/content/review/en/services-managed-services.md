# Technical Support & Operations

A solution retains value after delivery when operational, monitoring and update responsibilities are clear.

## When is this service relevant?

When your organization is seeking to improve its approach to technical support & operations, or is planning system changes and needs clear requirements before implementation. We start by reviewing the current environment, objectives, and dependencies.

## Potential scope

- Define covered systems
- Review support and escalation
- Assess monitoring, updates and backup
- Agree responsibilities and service levels

## Deliverables

Support and operations scope, escalation channels, and service levels as agreed.

## Our approach

We identify needs and constraints, present a clear scope for review, and agree implementation, handover, and responsibilities. Deliverables depend on project size and actual requirements; activities outside the agreed proposal are not included.
