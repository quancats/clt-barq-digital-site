# BIM | Secure enterprise communication, in one platform

A Barq Digital platform that helps organizations unify communication and collaboration channels, connecting teams in a digital experience shaped by administrative, security, and integration needs.

**Eyebrow:** Barq's flagship product · **Buttons:** Request a demo → /en/contact/demo/ · Visit BIM → https://bimchat.sa/ar/

## Why BIM?

When conversations, files, and notifications are scattered across applications, organizing information and managing access becomes more complex. BIM offers a more connected approach to enterprise communication, placing organizational needs at the center of the experience.

## An experience that connects communication and teamwork

- **Messaging:** Direct and group communication for exchanging messages and information in a work context.
- **Meetings:** Communication and meeting tools that support coordination across teams and locations.
- **File sharing:** Exchange documents within conversations to facilitate collaboration.
- **Enterprise announcements:** Organize general messages and administrative communications according to available permissions and settings.
- **Projects:** Organize communication around initiatives and teams according to the capabilities of the approved release.

## Administration aligned with organizational structure

The platform is designed for an enterprise work environment, with capabilities to manage users, organize access, and define responsibilities. Available functions and suitable configurations are determined in each organization's technical proposal.

## Security, privacy, and control

BIM considers information protection and access management in its product design. Technical discussions cover applicable encryption types and scope, identity controls, retention policies, and administrator access boundaries, depending on the release and configuration. This does not imply absolute security or regulatory certification.

## Integration and deployment

We assess existing identity systems, applications, hosting needs, and data location requirements before identifying commercially available integration and deployment options appropriate to the organization. Custom integrations and on-premises deployment are subject to technical assessment and commercial agreement.
