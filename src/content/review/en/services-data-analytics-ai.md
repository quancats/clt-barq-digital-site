# Data Analytics & AI

Data becomes more valuable when it can inform business decisions while preserving privacy and quality.

## When is this service relevant?

When your organization is seeking to improve its approach to data analytics & ai, or is planning system changes and needs clear requirements before implementation. We start by reviewing the current environment, objectives, and dependencies.

## Potential scope

- Identify data sources and quality
- Assess indicators and dashboards
- Evaluate ai use cases
- Define risks, constraints and evaluation measures

## Deliverables

Analytics concept, data requirements and an evaluable pilot or solution scope.

## Our approach

We identify needs and constraints, present a clear scope for review, and agree implementation, handover, and responsibilities. Deliverables depend on project size and actual requirements; activities outside the agreed proposal are not included.
