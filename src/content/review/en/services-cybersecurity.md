# Cybersecurity

Protecting digital assets starts with understanding threats and the technology environment, then selecting controls appropriate to the business.

## When is this service relevant?

When your organization is seeking to improve its approach to cybersecurity, or is planning system changes and needs clear requirements before implementation. We start by reviewing the current environment, objectives, and dependencies.

## Potential scope

- Assess network, endpoint and data protection needs
- Select malware protection controls
- Manage access
- Assess encryption requirements
- Review security configurations

## Deliverables

Risk and priority overview, solution scope, implementation recommendations and agreed acceptance criteria.

## Our approach

We identify needs and constraints, present a clear scope for review, and agree implementation, handover, and responsibilities. Deliverables depend on project size and actual requirements; activities outside the agreed proposal are not included.
