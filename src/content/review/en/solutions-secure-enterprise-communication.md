# Secure Enterprise Communication

Fragmented conversations and files make information management and team coordination harder.

## How do we address the challenge?

We start with organizational objectives and the current environment, then assess the following to identify an appropriate solution:

- Unify work channels
- Organize group and direct communication
- Define access needs
- Assess bim against the technical environment

## Expected value

More organized communication, clearer information-sharing paths, and a user-management approach. Target outcomes and verification measures are defined during scoping, based on organizational requirements.
