# Data Protection & Sovereignty

Organizations need to understand where data resides, how it moves, and who can access it.

## How do we address the challenge?

We start with organizational objectives and the current environment, then assess the following to identify an appropriate solution:

- Classify data needs
- Assess hosting and location
- Define access, encryption and retention
- Review processing responsibilities

## Expected value

A reviewable approach to data governance, controls and deployment choices. Target outcomes and verification measures are defined during scoping, based on organizational requirements.
