# Governance, Risk & Compliance

Organizations need to understand responsibilities and controls before selecting security tools. We help structure discussions around risks, policies, and applicable compliance requirements.

## When is this service relevant?

When your organization is seeking to improve its approach to governance, risk & compliance, or is planning system changes and needs clear requirements before implementation. We start by reviewing the current environment, objectives, and dependencies.

## Potential scope

- Identify assets and roles
- Review policies and controls
- Identify gaps
- Prioritize remediation

## Deliverables

Requirements and gap matrix, prioritized recommendations; not a certification or formal audit.

## Our approach

We identify needs and constraints, present a clear scope for review, and agree implementation, handover, and responsibilities. Deliverables depend on project size and actual requirements; activities outside the agreed proposal are not included.
