# Digital Workplace

Teams need access to the tools and information that support their work without unnecessary switching between systems.

## How do we address the challenge?

We start with organizational objectives and the current environment, then assess the following to identify an appropriate solution:

- Assess workplace tools
- Organize communication, meetings and announcements
- Identify integration opportunities
- Align employee experience with organizational controls

## Expected value

A more connected workplace, fewer fragmented touchpoints, and clear operational responsibilities. Target outcomes and verification measures are defined during scoping, based on organizational requirements.
