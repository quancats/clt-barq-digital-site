# Software Engineering & Business Applications

Effective enterprise applications address a clear need and fit existing workflows and systems.

## When is this service relevant?

When your organization is seeking to improve its approach to software engineering & business applications, or is planning system changes and needs clear requirements before implementation. We start by reviewing the current environment, objectives, and dependencies.

## Potential scope

- Requirements and user experience analysis
- Web, mobile and api design
- Implementation of agreed functionality
- Testing and handover

## Deliverables

Scope document, interface designs, software, test deliverables and documentation as agreed.

## Our approach

We identify needs and constraints, present a clear scope for review, and agree implementation, handover, and responsibilities. Deliverables depend on project size and actual requirements; activities outside the agreed proposal are not included.
