# Successful digital transformation starts with the process

New systems do not automatically create value; they must connect to workflows and organizational objectives.

## Understand work as it happens today

Document process start and end points, roles and tools. Identify repeated data entry, approval delays and manual information transfers.

## Define a measurable outcome

Choose an objective such as reducing duplicate entry, improving information access or clarifying request status. Define before-and-after measurement without assuming results.

## Design a connected experience

Decide what existing systems can improve and what requires development or integration. Consider usability, security, maintenance and user training.

## Implement incrementally

Start with a limited scope, test with users, and expand after addressing issues. Define operating and improvement responsibilities so benefits can continue after delivery.
