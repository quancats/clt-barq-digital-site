# Systems Integration & Identity

Organizations operate across multiple systems; integration becomes essential when information and access need to remain consistent.

## When is this service relevant?

When your organization is seeking to improve its approach to systems integration & identity, or is planning system changes and needs clear requirements before implementation. We start by reviewing the current environment, objectives, and dependencies.

## Potential scope

- Inventory systems and interfaces
- Identify data and identity sources
- Assess apis and authentication
- Design integration and failure handling

## Deliverables

Integration design, data and interface contracts, security and testing requirements within scope.

## Our approach

We identify needs and constraints, present a clear scope for review, and agree implementation, handover, and responsibilities. Deliverables depend on project size and actual requirements; activities outside the agreed proposal are not included.
