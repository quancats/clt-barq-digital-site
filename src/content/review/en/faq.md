# Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about Barq, its services, products, and getting started.

## What does Barq Digital offer?

We work in cybersecurity, IT and digital transformation, and offer BIM within our enterprise product direction. Service scope is defined before contracting.

## Is BIM a Barq product?

Yes. BIM is owned by Barq Digital and has its own brand and website.

## Can we request a demo?

Yes, through the BIM demo request page. We discuss available functions and your requirements.

## Do you deliver custom solutions?

Requirements, integrations and constraints are assessed before feasibility and a proposal are confirmed.

## Do you support on-premises deployment?

Deployment options are subject to technical assessment, availability and commercial agreement.

## Are the services or BIM regulator-certified?

Relevant available information and evidence can be provided on request. Marketing content is not a certification or license.

## How is pricing determined?

Based on agreed scope, users, integration, deployment and support.

## How do I request support?

Contact us and identify the product or project. Support channels and service levels depend on the applicable agreement.

## Can we partner with Barq?

We welcome proposals via the partnerships page and assess each opportunity individually.
