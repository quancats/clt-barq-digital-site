# Technology Consulting

Technology consulting turns fragmented requirements into actionable decisions while clarifying options and trade-offs.

## When is this service relevant?

When your organization is seeking to improve its approach to technology consulting, or is planning system changes and needs clear requirements before implementation. We start by reviewing the current environment, objectives, and dependencies.

## Potential scope

- Review the current environment
- Discuss business goals
- Evaluate alternatives
- Prioritize implementation
- Define scope and dependencies

## Deliverables

Clear recommendations, compared options and practical next steps.

## Our approach

We identify needs and constraints, present a clear scope for review, and agree implementation, handover, and responsibilities. Deliverables depend on project size and actual requirements; activities outside the agreed proposal are not included.
