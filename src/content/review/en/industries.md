# Technology that considers different industry needs

Organizations differ in workflows, data sensitivity and systems. We focus on understanding each sector's context before proposing a solution.

## Target industries

- **[Government & Public Sector](/en/industries/government/):** Public-sector organizations need reliable work channels that consider information governance, diverse departments and integration needs.
- **[Enterprises & Corporations](/en/industries/enterprise/):** Organizations with multiple departments and branches face coordination challenges, fragmented information and duplicated systems.
- **[Financial Services](/en/industries/financial-services/):** Financial environments depend on controlled information access, separation of responsibilities and operational risk management.
- **[Healthcare](/en/industries/healthcare/):** Healthcare teams and support departments need effective coordination while considering information sensitivity and individual privacy.
- **[Education & Training](/en/industries/education/):** Educational organizations need coordinated communication across departments, teams and sites, with role-based information access.
- **[Industrial & Operational Sectors](/en/industries/industrial/):** Multi-site operational environments rely on team coordination and organized information flow while considering business continuity.

## Every organization has distinct requirements

Regulatory, security, and technical requirements are assessed for each project.
