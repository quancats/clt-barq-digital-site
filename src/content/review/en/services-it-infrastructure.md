# IT Infrastructure

A connected, manageable technology infrastructure is the foundation of digital applications and services.

## When is this service relevant?

When your organization is seeking to improve its approach to it infrastructure, or is planning system changes and needs clear requirements before implementation. We start by reviewing the current environment, objectives, and dependencies.

## Potential scope

- Analyze existing networks and systems
- Identify connectivity, compute and storage needs
- Assess capacity and availability
- Plan implementation or modernization

## Deliverables

Proposed architecture, capacity and operational requirements, and implementation and testing plan as contracted.

## Our approach

We identify needs and constraints, present a clear scope for review, and agree implementation, handover, and responsibilities. Deliverables depend on project size and actual requirements; activities outside the agreed proposal are not included.
