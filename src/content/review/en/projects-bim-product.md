# BIM | From communication needs to an enterprise platform

The story of a Barq Digital-owned product focused on organizing communication and collaboration in enterprise workplaces.

**Type:** Owned product · **Domain:** Enterprise communication and collaboration

## The challenge

Many organizations use separate channels for messaging, meetings and information sharing. This can fragment work context and complicate access management.

## The solution concept

BIM offers a unified experience bringing communication channels together while considering administrative requirements. The product concept connects user experience with identity, data, and integration needs.

## Product pillars

Messaging, meetings, file sharing, announcements, user management, and integrations subject to technical assessment and release availability.

## Intended value

Organized work information, easier team coordination, and less reliance on fragmented channels. Actual outcomes are measured when evaluating the product in an organization's environment.
