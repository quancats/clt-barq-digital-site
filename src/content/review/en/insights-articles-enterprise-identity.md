# Enterprise identity starts with the employee lifecycle

A strong password is not enough if accounts and permissions fail to reflect changing employee responsibilities.

## Joining and role assignment

Define the authoritative identity source and account creation process. Assign permissions according to actual roles rather than copying another user's access without review.

## Changing responsibilities

When an employee changes departments, review old and new permissions. Documented roles and approvals help reduce accumulated unnecessary access.

## Removing access

Define a clear process for disabling accounts and revoking sessions and tokens when an engagement ends, while retaining information under approved policies.

## Continuous review

Assign responsibility for reviewing accounts and permissions. Test onboarding, role changes and departure scenarios. Assess multifactor authentication and event logging where appropriate to risk.
