This policy explains the handling of personal data related to visiting the Barq Digital website and contacting us through it. It does not cover BIM operations or customer data processing, which are governed by product documentation and applicable contracts.

## Responsible entity

Barq Digital for Information Technology, based in Dammam, Saudi Arabia, is responsible for personal data processing on the corporate website. For privacy inquiries and requests, email [info@barqdigital.sa](mailto:info@barqdigital.sa).

## Data collected

The forms ask for a name, email address and request details. Depending on the request type, they may also ask for an organization name, a service, an estimated user range or a partnership type. The forms also record acceptance of the privacy policy. The site uses Cloudflare Turnstile to verify submissions, and the form handler uses a hash of the IP address to limit repeated submissions.

## Purposes and legal basis

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, assess your needs, prepare a proposal or arrange follow-up. Required fields are needed to process the request. You can leave optional fields blank. We use technical data to protect the website and diagnose faults, and request separate consent for optional analytics.

## Sharing and processing location

Barq uses Cloudflare to host the website, verify submissions, and deliver them to the company inbox used to respond. When Google Analytics is enabled with your consent, Google processes website usage data. Depending on provider settings, data may be processed outside Saudi Arabia. Transfers are subject to applicable legal requirements.

## Retention and security

We delete inquiry data when it is no longer needed and no later than 12 months after closing the inquiry, unless a legal obligation or the management of an ongoing contractual relationship requires longer retention.

## Individual rights

You may ask how your data is used, request access or a copy, request correction or destruction, and withdraw consent where applicable, subject to legal conditions. Send requests or complaints to the privacy email above. We may ask for information to verify your identity before acting on a request. You may also submit a complaint through the National Data Governance Platform.

## Cookies

The website uses browser local storage to remember your appearance and analytics preferences. If Google Analytics is enabled, website measurement begins after you consent. You can change your choice through the privacy settings in the footer.

## Updates and contact

The policy may change when processing or legal requirements change. The effective date is provided upon publication.
