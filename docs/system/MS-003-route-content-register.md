WP-003.2 · REQ-003 · v1.0 · Evidence reviewed 2026-11-25

## Route content register

Both Arabic and English require independent editorial approval. P0 identifies mandatory release content. P1 requires a template and data capability, with publication conditional on approved material. PG-022 also requires its partnership form capability. Missing public content for a P0 page blocks release. A P1 section without approved content requires explicit owner approval for suppression and stays out of navigation and sitemap.

## Approval and handoff

1. The content owner creates an item for each selected route instance or asset with PG ID, stable item ID, source reference, language, priority, ownership role and revision. Store source permissions and sensitive records with authorized owners. Record only sanctioned references and decisions here.
2. The relevant approvers review corporate and product facts, authorized use, translations and the exact public revision. For each decision, record the affected item and locale, approved revision, status, approver role, decision date and evidence reference. No item-level publication approvals are present in this register.
3. Engineering consumes approved content for build-time publication. Preview fixtures, drafts and blocked material remain outside production output. Each published translation must exist and be approved before linking corresponding alternate pages. The local article rules for drafts and trusted MDX are recorded in the [content ownership map](../astro/CONTENT-OWNERSHIP.md).
4. Use the legacy chrome assets as references for PG-001 and shared branding, project imagery for PG-011/012, blog imagery for PG-014/015, and the legacy contact form and embedded map as processing and rights inputs for PG-019/024. The inventory records unresolved origin status, deep-link and asset coverage, redirects and Search Console evidence. Editorial reuse and final redirects depend on approved equivalents and documented rights. WP-003.2 remains blocked for the corporate, taxonomy, BIM claim, rights, legal, recipient and localization decisions documented above.
