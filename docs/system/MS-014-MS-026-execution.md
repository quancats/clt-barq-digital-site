## Content and routing handoff

- Use the translation key and slug `bim` for the flagship product in both languages. Other detail pages can use localized slugs. The approved sibling entry supplies the language switch destination.
- Approved product records may supply brand, platforms, use cases, FAQ and an official HTTPS URL. Services can supply a category, FAQ and related product or solution keys. Commercial and project links use approved entries in the same locale.
- Image records support localized captions, intrinsic dimensions and width-based variants. Every supplied source and derivative is checked as an approved public asset. Use the content rights and approval records when loading actual BIM media.
- The consultation route receives the approved service translation key in its CTA URL. Intake, allowlisted selection and verified delivery belong to the later forms work. The confirmation route displays a neutral state on direct access.
